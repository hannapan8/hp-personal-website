About me

Hi, I'm Hanna.

I'm a Bay Area native finishing my degree in Informatics at the University of Washington, with a focus on Data Science. I’m curious about how people use technology and how we can make it more useful and intuitive.

I think of myself as a horizontal thinker: I enjoy connecting user research, data, design, engineering, and business. That’s what draws me to product management: understanding a problem, making thoughtful tradeoffs, and working with a team to bring a solution to life.

I’ve explored that through improving internal sales workflows at Adobe, designing and building applications, and leading programming instruction at UW. Across those experiences, I’ve found that I do my best work when I’m connecting perspectives and turning complex problems into clear next steps.
